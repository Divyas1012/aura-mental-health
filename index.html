Reframing Wizard

Step 1: Automatic Negative Thought

Write down the distressing negative thought exactly as it occurred in your mind (e.g. "I'm going to fail my exam and ruin my life.")

Step 2: Identify Cognitive Distortions

Our minds often trick us. Select the bias/distortion that applies to this thought:

💥 Catastrophizing

Assuming the absolute worst-case scenario will happen.

🏁 All-or-Nothing

Viewing things in extremes. Perfect or failure.

🧠 Mind Reading

Assuming you know why others think negatively of you.

👤 Personalization

Blaming yourself entirely for things you can't control.

🔄 Overgeneralization

Assuming one setback is a never-ending cycle.

📢 Shoulds

Demanding absolute perfection, leading to heavy guilt.

Step 3: Develop a Balanced Perspective

Rewrite the thought by looking at the facts. Challenge the distortion. What is a more logical, compassionate, and realistic alternative?

Rebalanced Perspectives

Reviewing previously reframed thoughts reinforces logical thinking pathways.
